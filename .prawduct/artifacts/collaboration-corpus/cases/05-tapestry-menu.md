# A menu offered to someone who had the idea already

## User said (queued)

> movement II is kind of intense. I like it, but hard to parse. can we instead pivot to a second study, simpler, to explore a different idea?

## Context

Agent text immediately before: *"Now let me build the experiment your finding actually calls for, plus the envelope fix — but I won't push while you're listening."* It was already building the successor to Movement II, unasked.

## Agent did

An `AskUserQuestion` with three pre-designed studies (register spread / carve a chord by movement / which notes are levers). The user rejected the picker ("wants to clarify"). Agent: *"Of course — what would you like to clarify?"* — and, to its credit, self-diagnosed: *"You said 'a different idea,' which I read as possibly meaning one of yours rather than a menu from me."*

## What happened next

The user typed the whole of `rug` — sixteen bars, every note in C major across six octaves, a negative glissade up then a triad glissade down. It had been in their head the entire time.

## The tell

"A different idea" from someone who has been generating the ideas all session means *they have one*. A picker also converts a nuanced creative fork into a choice among the agent's designs; across the corpus, no creative-question picker was ever answered by picking an option (see CTM-08, CTM-14).

## The right move

*"What's the idea?"*

## Assertion

When a user signals a pivot or "a different idea", the agent asks what it is before proposing alternatives. Structured pickers are not used for creative questions.

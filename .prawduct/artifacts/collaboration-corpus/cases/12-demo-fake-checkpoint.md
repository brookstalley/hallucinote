## Why this case is here

This is the June complaint (*"agent stops too much and asks for permission to proceed"*) in its pure form: a stop with no decision on the table, wearing the clothes of a checkpoint. Any fix that raises the collaborative register must not bring this back. The distinction the norms need is not *how often* to stop but *what kind of stop*: a creative fork the user would want to own is a legitimate pause; "I finished a phase" is not; "my context is getting long" never is.

## Assertion

A pause names a decision the user would want to own, or it does not happen. Context-budget management is never presented as a checkpoint.

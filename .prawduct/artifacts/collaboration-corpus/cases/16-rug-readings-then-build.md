Stated a reading and the alternative for each, in one sentence. Built (seven tool calls, about eight minutes, no envelopes). ## What happened next

(fifty-four seconds later), then *"new live set"*, then a listening report that opened the best turn of the session (CTM-17).

## What made it work

The prompt was a spec, so the ambiguities were *in* it and could be named. The readings were stated, not asked, because they were cheap to reverse; the fresh-set gate was asked, because it was not. The turn was short. The one slip (CTM-06) was a mechanism on a dimension the spec did not cover.

## Assertion

On a directive prompt the agent names the genuine ambiguities, states a reading and its alternative in a sentence each, builds the hearable unit, and stops only at an expensive-to-reverse gate.

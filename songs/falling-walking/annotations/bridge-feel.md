# Bridge feel — "sideways step"

The bridge is a sneaky sideways step into a brighter room. Key center modulates to B♭ major — a chord the listener has already tasted (verse VI). Pivoting *to* B♭ as a key center feels organic, not jarring.

Classic bossa I-vi-ii-V loop: B♭maj7 → Gm7 → Cm7 → F7. Sophisticated, warm, contemplative.

## Pivot back

F7 → Dm is the bridge's exit. F7 is the V of B♭ inside the bridge, but its 7th (E♭) resolves down to D, and F is already III in Dm. Smooth re-entry into the next chorus.

## Production cues

- **Drop the aggressive pad entirely.** This is a sonic clue that the song has stepped sideways.
- **Electric piano (Rhodes-style)** for the maj7/min7 voicings — warm, sophisticated, electronic-acoustic.
- **Smooth synth bass with portamento** — fretless-ish, walking bossa lines.
- Soft electronic rim + light shaker (bossa cell at quiet level).
- Maybe a single sustained pad note for low-presence atmosphere.

The bridge is the listener's *breather* — it should sound like a different room temperature, not a different planet.

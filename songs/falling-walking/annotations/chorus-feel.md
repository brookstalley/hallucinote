# Chorus feel — "circular, finding"

The chorus *finds* what the verse was seeking, but only by climbing forever. Root motion F → C → G → D = four ascending fifths. The cycle of fifths is an infinite staircase; the loop snaps back to Dm and you climb again.

Equal 2-bar lengths = metronomic, circular feel. Half-time on top of calypso 16ths = floating over a busy underbed.

G (Dorian IV, B natural) brightens the chorus without modulating — this is the "finding," but it's harmonically borrowed, not earned. The brightness is conditional.

## Production cues

- **Pad** opens up vs verse: same patch but filter unmasks. Sonic version of "finding."
- **Bass**: shorter, plucky synth bass. Less sustained, supports calypso 16ths.
- **Calypso element**: glassy synth-marimba or steel-drum synth playing the 3-3-2 tresillo cell. Bright, high, wide stereo.
- **Counter-melody bell synth** for sparse high-register answering phrases.
- Half-time kick/snare grounds the float.

## C3' twist exception

C3' (bars 40-48) breaks the cycle — see `decisions/2026-05-01-c3-prime-twist.md`. The chorus's "finding" is conditional; the climax reveals that the loop was always going to break.

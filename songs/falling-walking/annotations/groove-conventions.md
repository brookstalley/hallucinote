# Groove conventions

Section-by-section groove vocabulary used across the song. Each section has a distinct rhythmic identity; the intro/outro reuses the verse pulse stripped down.

## Verse (trip-hop)

- Kick stumble: 1 + late 2.75 alt bars
- Snare 2+4 pushed +0.04 beats (laid-back)
- 8th hats with deep ghosts on offbeats (vel 35)

## Chorus (half-time + calypso)

- Kick on 1, snare on 3 (half-time)
- Hat tresillo (3-3-2 cell at beats 0, 0.75, 1.5, 2.0, 2.75, 3.5)
- Open hat lifts on every 2nd bar 3.75

## Bridge (bossa)

- Bossa kick (1, 1.5, 2.0, 3.5)
- Forward 3-2 clave on cross-stick (pitch 37) alternating per bar
- 16th shaker on closed hat (vel ~30-42)

## Intro / outro

- Stripped trip-hop kick + 4th hats
- Fade in/out by velocity ramp

## Drummer flourishes (v3 addition)

- Open-hat (46) lifts on bars 4/8/12 beat 3.5 — sectional accents on mini-fill bars
- Soft crash (49 vel 75) on bar 9 beat 1 (Gm chord arrival)
- Splash (55 vel 70) on bar 13 beat 1 (B♭ arrival)
- Ride-bell (53) ghost notes through bar 7 — texture variation deep in the long Dm
- Soft crash (49 vel 88) on chorus bar 1 beat 1 (chorus entry)

## Reusable principle

Three distinct rhythmic identities, one reused for the frame. Half-time chorus floats over calypso 16ths — the busy underbed makes the half-time feel intentional, not lazy. Bridge bossa is the quietest groove of the song; that's how it earns its key-center shift.

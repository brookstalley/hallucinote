# "Powerful electronic" production tricks

The aesthetic is "unabashedly electronic, powerful" — not EDM-pumping, not chillout-passive. The power comes from layered tension + selective dynamics, not loudness.

## Core tricks

- **Sidechain pad + bass to kick** (subtle, not EDM pumping). Subtle pump = "breathing" weight.
- **Saturation on bass + pad** for harmonic richness.
- **Wide stereo on pads, mono bass below ~150Hz.** Classic low-end discipline; pads carry width, bass carries weight.
- **Reserve transient peaks for chorus.** The chorus arrival should *feel* louder even when peak levels are matched.

## Sound design intent (synth patches)

All synths currently at factory default presets — sound design pass pending. Targets:

- **Verse pad**: dark detuned supersaw with grit (slow attack, dark low-pass, subtle distortion + chorus).
- **Synth bass**: warm with harmonic bite, slow portamento between root changes (the portamento itself sounds like falling).
- **Chorus pluck**: glassy steel-drum-style, plays the tresillo cell, bright and high.
- **Bell**: FM bell for sparse high-register answering phrases.
- **Bridge EP**: Rhodes-style, warm, sophisticated.
- **Chiptune lead**: square/pulse wave with slight bitcrush, sits in mid-high register.

## Why subtle over heavy

The brief is "powerful electronic, not EDM" — heavy sidechain pumping would shift the song toward dance-floor energy. The intent is *brooding power*. Sidechain creates rhythmic "breathing" without making the song aggressive; saturation adds richness without making it loud.

# Verse feel — "falling, seeking"

The verse is sad, like weight getting worse. Each section's harmonic rhythm tells the story:

- 8 bars on Dm = the "seeking" — ear hunts for movement that won't come.
- Harmonic rhythm accelerates (8 → 4 → 2 → 1 bars per chord). The floor gives way faster as the verse progresses.
- Bass walks D → G → B♭ → A. The iv (Gm) adds weight before the descent — darker than a plain tetrachord.
- A (V) in bar 15 yanks back to Dm. No exit.

## Production cues

- **Pad** should be oppressive, dark, sit at the back. Detuned supersaw, dark low-pass filter, slow attack, subtle distortion + chorus. Optional granular layer for unease.
- **Bass** should *slide* between root changes — portamento itself sounds like falling.
- **Texture**: vinyl crackle, sparse ride bell ghost notes, reversed swells into bar 1.
- Sidechain pad to kick (subtle pump = "breathing" weight).

The aesthetic is brooding, not bombastic. Power comes from the held tension, not loud peaks.

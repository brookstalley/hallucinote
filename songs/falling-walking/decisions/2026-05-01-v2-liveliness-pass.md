# v2 liveliness pass

Strategic move from v1: the song was structurally complete (concept + tracks + clips + arrangement) but felt mechanical because every part played the same content every bar. v2 added per-bar variation across drums, bass, and pad to make the song feel like a band playing rather than a sequencer looping.

## Why

A static loop reads as a sketch even when the chord progression is interesting. Liveliness — small variations, fills, dynamic articulation — is what separates a demo from a track. The harmonic and structural decisions were settled; this pass made them *feel* like a performance.

## Manifestations

| Area | Change |
|------|--------|
| Verse drums | 16th snare mini-fills on bar 4/8/12; ghost kicks on offbeats; real drum fill bar 14 (toms 47/50, 32nd snare roll, crash 49 on lift) |
| Verse bass | Follows kick stumble (root 1 + late 2.75); octave-up answer on bar 4 of every other bar; bar 15 = 8th-note drive into chorus |
| Verse pad | Top voice walks D4→E4→F4→E4 across bars 1-8 of long Dm; chord stabs on bar 4 + 8 + 12 beat 4 (re-articulation — later removed in v3, see chorus-pad-no-stabs) |
| Chorus drums | Ghost snare on 2.75; occasional ghost kick on 1.5 |
| Chorus bass | Full tresillo (3-3-2) per chord — locks bass to calypso identity |
| Chorus pad | Tresillo stab on beat 0.75 of each bar |
| Bridge bass | Walking bossa with passing tones (Bb-C-D-F, G-Bb-C-D, C-Eb-F-G, F-A-C-D) |
| Intro drums | Progressive hat journey: tick on 1 (b1-4) → bossa 16th shaker (b5-8) → tresillo (b9-12) → trip-hop 8ths (b13-16). Open hat lift on bar 16 last 16th |
| Intro arp (NEW track 9) | Foreshadows verse 8/4/2/1 progression: Dm sparse (1-4) → Dm 8ths (5-8) → Gm 8ths "iv reveal" (9-12) → Bb 16ths (13-14) → A held (15) → silence (16) |
| Intro bell (NEW track 10) | Sparse high arpeggio bars 5-8 (D5/F5/A5/D5) |
| Intro lead | Hook fragment bars 9-12 (4 notes), full hook bars 13-16 |
| C3' drums (NEW slot 8 track 5) | Chorus pattern + multi-flavor crashes: Crash 1 on bar 1 (arrival), Splash on bar 3 (Fmaj7 lift), Crash 2 on bar 5 (Cmaj7 darker), China on bar 7 (B♭maj7 TWIST — trashy match for harmonic surprise), Crash 1 on bar 8.875 (push to outro) |
| Outro pad | Re-articulation stab at bar 5 mid-fade |

## What it changes

The arrangement now sounds like a band rather than a loop. The intro builds tension via the progressive hat journey + foreshadowing arp; the verse drums add fills and ghosts; the chorus locks the calypso identity to the bass; C3' gets its own crash signature for the climax. The pad stabs were a v2 idea later proven counterproductive (see `chorus-pad-no-stabs` for the v3 correction).

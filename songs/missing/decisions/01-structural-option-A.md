# 01 — Structural option: root arrives only at the end (Option A)

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Decided by:** user (chosen from three options A/B/C)
**Status:** locked

## Question

How does the missing root relate to the song over time — never appear, appear at a
chorus, or appear once at the end?

## Decision

**Option A.** The entire song floats rootless. The root appears exactly once, at the
very end (the coda), swelling in over ~4 bars. This is the first and only grounding.

Rejected:
- **Option B** (rootless verse / rooted chorus) — too listenable, too comfortable.
  Hands the ground back every 30s; betrays a song whose identity is *missing*.
- **Option C** (root never appears) — the purest "missing," but this piece is a
  *lost → found* arc, and found requires the root to land. C is reserved for a
  future variation (see `decisions/09-variation-roadmap.md`).

## Rationale

The theme is lost → **found**, with found earned only at the end. Option A is the
only structure that delivers a real arrival while withholding it long enough to make
the arrival devastating. The reveal is the song's entire emotional payload; Option A
spends it once, at the right moment. See `annotations/00-theme.md`.

## Consequences

- The sub-bass octave is reserved exclusively for the reveal (`decisions/03`).
- Choruses must lift without resolving (`annotations/00-theme.md`).
- The notch-LFO and arrangement build toward a single release, not repeated ones.

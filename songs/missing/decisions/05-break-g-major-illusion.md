# 05 — The break: the G-major illusion (false summit) → B7 pivot

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Decided by:** user (chose "into the G-major illusion" over "up a 5th to B" and "sketch both")
**Status:** locked

## Decision

The break modulates **into G major** — the bright phantom key that rootless E-minor
has secretly implied all along — and floats there *still rootless* (G never sounds).
It is the **false summit**: the most energetic, euphoric section, where the narrator
believes they've found their purpose. Then the pivot chord **B7** reintroduces D#
(foreign to G), collapses the illusion, and lands the break on the **V of E (B)**,
setting up the reveal (B7 → Em).

## Why G major (the thematic payoff)

The song's core duality is "E minor vs. its phantom G major." The break stops
resisting and *inhabits* the illusion. And there's a built-in irony: a **major key
voiced rootless drifts darker on the surface** (Gmaj7→Bm, Cmaj7→Em). So the break is
harmonically the brightest place in the song while its rootless surface still leans
minor — **even the false dawn has the wound in it.** The listener feels lifted but
senses something's still wrong. Exactly what a false summit should feel like.

## The pivot (how it lands "back on the 5")

**B7 belongs to both keys:** it is **V7/vi in G major** (the secondary dominant that
tonicizes Em) *and* the **V in E harmonic minor.** Introducing B7 at the break's end
brings back **D#**, which is foreign to G — the audible signal that the illusion is
ending. Rootless B7 = D#dim; that D# leading tone deposits us on the dominant of the
real home. This satisfies the user's brief exactly: *key change + same rootless trick,
resolving back onto the 5 of the original key as the break ends.*

## Rootless surface inside the break (reference)

G-major diatonic 7ths, rootless: Gmaj7→**Bm**, Cmaj7→**Em**, D7→**F#dim**,
Em7→**G**, Am7→**C**, Bm7→**D**. Keep the brightness in arrangement/register/energy;
the surface triads stay minor-leaning by nature, which is the point.

## Consequences

- The break is the dynamic/energy peak of the song; the reveal is the *emotional*
  peak. Don't let the break out-resolve the coda — it must feel like winning then
  losing, never like arriving.
- B7 is the hinge: compose the last bars of the break to spotlight D# before the coda.

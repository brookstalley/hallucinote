# 07 — Tempo and feel

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Decided by:** user
**Status:** locked

## Decision

- **100 BPM, 4/4.**
- **Energetic** overall — driven by a **pulsing arp** (8th/16th motion).
- The intended contrast: *an energetic song with one structural "problem"* — the
  missing root/floor. The energy makes the absence more unsettling, not less: the
  song moves with confidence toward a ground that isn't there.

## Rationale

A slow, spacious treatment would let the float read as ambient/pretty and dissolve
the tension. At 100 with an insistent arp, the *yearning becomes bodily* — the
listener is carried forward, reaching, which makes the withheld resolution ache more.
The arp also trains the ear on the available chord tones through repetition, so the
listener can track the harmony even with the ground missing.

## Consequences

- Arp is a core, ever-present layer (not decoration); it carries energy and
  chord-tone information.
- Rhythm/drum design should sustain energy while *also* echoing the "missing
  foundation" theme — see `decisions/08-rhythm.md` (under discussion).

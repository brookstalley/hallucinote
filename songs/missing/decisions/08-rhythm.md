## The core parallel

The harmonic floor (the root) is withheld all song and returns once, at the reveal.
The rhythm does the **same thing** with the rhythmic floor: **beat 3** — the body's
secondary downbeat, where the floor reasserts in the back half of the bar — is
withheld and returns at the same instant. Two foundations, one release. This is the
theme rendered a second time, in time instead of pitch (see `annotations/00-theme.md`).

## Beat 3 — a THREE-STATE system mirroring the harmonic arc

| Section | Beat-3 state | Mirrors |
|---|---|---|
| Verses / choruses (drums in) | **Absence** — no kick on beat 3 | rootless / lost |
| Break (false summit) | **False fill** — a bright, high accent (crash / cymbal swell / impact) lands on beat 3. Fills the hole, feels like arrival, but is the WRONG instrument and carries NO sub. | the G-major illusion: a fake floor, present-but-false |
| Coda (reveal) | **Real kick on 3, first time** — and it stays from here | the root returns / found |

The break's false-fill is the rhythmic counterpart of the false summit: just as the
break gives a fake harmonic home (bright G major, still rootless), it gives a fake
rhythmic floor (a bright crash on 3, not the real kick, no sub). **Bright lie, then
deep truth.** The real kick's first appearance on beat 3 is reserved for the reveal
exactly like the root's — restoration is **once and terminal.**

## LOCKED invariants (any pattern must satisfy all of these)

1. **No kick on beat 3** from the moment drums enter (verse 1) until the coda reveal.
2. **Restoration once and terminal:** the first real kick on beat 3 is the reveal;
   from there it stays as the new grounded state.
3. **The hole must be FELT.** Frame beat 3 with activity around it so its emptiness
   reads as intentional withholding, not generic syncopation (e.g. accents on the
   "and of 2" / "and of 3" bracketing the gap, or a backbeat that conspicuously skips 3).
4. **Backbone on 2 & 4** stays strong — the backbeat carries the energy so missing-3
   reads as deliberate, not limp.
5. **Clear downbeat on beat 1** each bar — the song is disoriented about the back-half
   floor, not about where the bar begins.
6. **Low end reserved (ties to `decisions/03`, `decisions/04`).** The kick is
   **sub-rolled-off / mid-focused** until the reveal — no deep sub energy in the bottom
   octave (<~100 Hz) before the coda. Drums are present-but-weightless, like the bass.
   The reveal is a **triple convergence:** sub-osc E floods the bottom octave **+** the
   kick regains its weight **+** the kick lands on beat 3 **+** the harmony resolves —
   all in one instant.
7. **Break false-fill is bright, not sub** — crash/cymbal/impact, never the kick timbre,
   never sub-bearing (the bottom octave stays saved for the true reveal).
8. **Arp pulse is constant** — forward motion independent of the kick pattern.
9. **NOT four-on-the-floor-minus-3.** The pattern must be characterful/syncopated.
10. **Tempo/feel:** 100 BPM, 4/4, energetic (`decisions/07`).

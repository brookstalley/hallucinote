# 09 — Variation roadmap (this is the first of several)

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Decided by:** user (stated intent to do several variations on the theme)
**Status:** informational — captures deferred directions so v1 stays focused

"missing" (this song) is **variation 1** of a planned body of work exploring the
rootless-voicing constraint from different emotional angles. Decisions in this song
deliberately defer richer ideas to keep v1 legible. Candidates for future variations:

- **Option C — root never appears.** The most committed version; denies resolution
  as a statement. (This song is Option A.)
- **Major-key inversion.** Write in a major key so the rootless surface drifts
  *darker* — melancholy hiding inside something meant to be bright. (We taste this
  in the break here; a whole piece deserves it.)
- **Dorian / natural-minor** colorings for a softer or chromatically different feel.
- **Total melodic root-avoidance** (vs. this song's "graze but never land").
- **Per-chord notch LFO** with the tonic most suppressed ("home most denied"),
  Q-as-second-axis, and an **accelerating LFO** turning the reveal into a gradual
  process. (v1 keeps the notch simple — `decisions/10`.)
- **Sub-oscillator ghost root** buried below perception throughout, opening at the
  reveal from inside the sound (vs. v1's fully-reserved-then-revealed sub octave).
- **Whole-tone / diminished** symmetrical-scale treatment — a harmonic hall of
  mirrors. High risk; attempt after the technique is internalized.
- **Backward composition:** write the *perceived* (phantom) progression as the
  primary target and derive the "real" roots from it.

# 14 — Chorus voicings (synthpop: dazzling arp + backbeat stabs)

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Decided by:** user ("everything here looks fantastic"; explicitly preserve the
bar-3 E pass) · agreed-after-confirm
**Status:** locked (spec). Applies to chorus1, chorus2, and finalchorus (variant below).

## Harmonic loop — `i – V – iv – V`, rootless, never cadences

4-bar loop ×4 over the 16-bar chorus. Ending each loop on the **V** (D# leading tone
screaming toward an E that never comes) means the chorus has the *shape* of resolution
(iv–V–i motion) with none of the substance. Perpetual pull. This is the chorus's
harmonic identity vs. the verse (which drifts i↔iv and barely touches the V — see
`decisions/15`): the **V's deployment is itself an arc** — scarce in the verse (numb/
lost), relentless in the chorus (reaching), resolved only in the coda (found).

| Bar | Chord (rootless) | Shell | Phantom surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | i — EmM7, remove E | G B D# | G augmented |
| 2 | V — B7, remove B | D# F# A | D# dim |
| 3 | iv — Am7, remove A | C E G | C major |
| 4 | V | D# F# A | D# dim |

## The dazzling arp — 16th cascades, ~G3→F#5, surprise note at each apex

| Bar | Chord | Arp note pool | The dazzle / surprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | i | G B D# F# A C | **D#** (maj7 leading tone) rung at the peak; **C** (b13) wistful upper grace; suspension **A→G** (4–3 sigh) |
| 2 | V | D# F# A **C** G | **C = b9** (flamenco-dominant bite); **G** = #5 ache. The "pull" bar. |
| 3 | iv | C E **G** B + *E pass* | = **Cmaj7 shimmer** — the bright pocket; **B** (9th) twinkles up top |
| 4 | V | (as bar 2) | ends hot, unresolved |

### THE KEY RULE — E-free everywhere except a graze in bar 3

The arp is **completely E-free in bars 1, 2, 4.** It sounds lush and *complete* but
never plays the one note that would ground it. **E is permitted only in bar 3** (the iv),
where it is the 5th of Am, not a tonic (`decisions/04`) — and even there only
**mid-register, in passing, never the lowest note, never held.** The listener hears a
dazzling, full arp and cannot name what's missing. This is the rootless trick rendered
in the song's most prominent, most "arrival-shaped" layer — the lie at its shiniest is
exactly where it's emptiest. **(User-flagged: preserve this bar-3 E pass — it's the
tease the coda reveal pays off.)**

## Backbeat stabs (punch 2 & 4 under the arp) — close mid voicings

```
i :  G3  B3  D#4   (+ D#5 sheen)
V :  F#3 A3  D#4   (+ C4 on accents = the b9 color)
iv:  C4  E4  G4    (+ B4 maj7 shimmer)
V :  F#3 A3  D#4
```

Top voice strides **D#4 → D#4 → E4 → D#4** — the leading tone gripped and never released
except one bar's lift to E (the only chord where E is safe), then yanked back. The hook
*is* the unreleased D#.

## finalchorus variant (bars 97–112)

Same loop + arp, **intensified** (add an upper-octave arp layer / extra pad weight) as
the last reach. End the section on the **V (B7)** with maximum D# tension — this hands
directly to the coda, where that D# finally resolves up to a rooted E (`decisions/17`).

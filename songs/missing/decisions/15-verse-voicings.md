# 15 — Verse voicings (cinematic: sustained open pads)

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Decided by:** user (verse = "more pads and more sustained"; cinematic idiom) ·
agreed-after-confirm
**Status:** locked (spec). Applies to verse1, verse2, and verse3 (variant below).

## Idiom & role

Cinematic (`decisions/13`): atmospheric, sustained, **open/ambiguous voicings** — no
dense extensions that would clarify the key. Pads-forward; the recessed/filtered arp
threads underneath (`decisions/08` inv. 8). This is where the **notch-LFO is most
audible** (`decisions/10`) — the sustained pad gives the root-suppression filter
something to chew on. The tresillo+backbeat groove still drives underneath, so the verse
is *cinematic-but-moving* (M83-style), not slow.

## Harmonic motion — drift between i and iv; V saved for the turnaround

The verse **floats between the unstable phantom-i (G+) and the consoling phantom-iv
(C major)** and barely touches the dominant. This is the deliberate contrast with the
chorus's relentless V (`decisions/14`): the verse is numb/lost, so the *reaching* V is
withheld until the very end of the section as a 2-bar lift into the chorus.

16-bar layout: **i (4) – iv (4) – i (4) – iv (2) – V (2)** → lifts into the chorus.

## Pad voicings — open, spread ~G3–D#5, quartal/quintal where possible

| Chord (rootless) | Open pad voicing | Color |
|---|---|---|
| i — Em (G B D#) | **G3 – B3 – F#4 – D#5** | b3 / 5 / **9** / maj7 — airy; the 9th (F#) opens it, the maj7 (D#) floats on top, keeping the leading-tone ache present even in the drift |
| iv — Am (C E G) | **G3 – C4 – E4 – B4** | = Cmaj9-no-root — the bright consoling pocket, gentle; **E is an inner voice only** (5th of Am, light, never lowest/loudest — `decisions/04`) |
| V — B7 (D# F# A) | **F#3 – A3 – D#4 – C5** | b7 / 5(no, F# is 5)… 5 / b7 / 3 / **b9** — the b9 (C) up top is the cinematic ache; used only as the 2-bar turnaround lift |

(Bass plays the 5ths below — B2 / E2 / F#2, hi-passed, weightless — `decisions/04`.)

## verse3 variant (bars 89–96) — the disabuse

Half-length (8 bars) and **stripped/cold** — this is where the false summit is revealed
as a lie. Pare the pad down (drop voices, expose it), drop arrangement energy, let the
notch open wide again (root maximally absent). Same i↔iv drift but bleaker and more
exposed; no euphoria left. Hands into the finalchorus (the last reach).

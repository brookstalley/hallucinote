# 16 — Break voicings (the G-major illusion → B7 pivot)

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Decided by:** agreed-after-confirm (realizes the framework in `decisions/05`)
**Status:** locked (spec). Break = bars 73–88. The false summit.

## Idiom & shape

The euphoric false summit (`decisions/05`, `decisions/13`): **synthpop at its biggest**,
then a collapse. The break modulates **into G major** — the phantom key rootless E-minor
has implied all along — still **rootless** (G never sounds). Bright register, dazzling
arp foregrounded, full energy; beat-3 false-fill is a **bright crash** (`decisions/08`).
Then the **B7 pivot** reintroduces D#, foreign to G, and the genre morphs synthpop →
cinematic as the illusion collapses, depositing us on the V of E for verse3's return.

## The euphoric loop — G major `I – V – vi – IV`, rootless

The most anthemic pop loop, voiced rootless. Note the irony (`decisions/05`): a **major
key voiced rootless drifts darker** — the bright G framework surfaces as minor/dim. The
euphoria lives in the register, energy, and arrangement; the rootless surface still
carries the wound. *Even the false dawn isn't clean.*

| Chord (rootless) | Shell | Phantom surface | Open voicing (~B3–B5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — Gmaj7, remove G | B D F# | B minor | **B3 – D4 – F#4 – A4** (3/5/7/9) |
| V — D7, remove D | F# A C | F# dim | **F#3 – A3 – C4 – E4** (3/5/b7/9) |
| vi — Em7, remove E | G B D | **G major** | **G3 – B3 – D4 – F#4** (the one bar that surfaces bright G) |
| IV — Cmaj7, remove C | E G B | E minor | **E3 – G3 – B3 – D4** (3/5/7/9) |

Run the loop for the bulk of the break (bars ~73–84), building.

## The pivot — B7 (bars ~85–88)

**B7 rootless = D# – F# – A.** D# is **foreign to G major** — the audible signal the
illusion is ending. B7 is the hinge: it is V7/vi *in G* (it tonicizes Em) AND the **V in
E harmonic minor**, so it belongs to both keys and lands us cleanly back on home's
dominant.

> Pivot voicing: **D#4 – F#4 – A4 – C5** (3 / 5 / b7 / **b9**). The D# stabs in (the
> leading tone, missing all break, returns); the b9 (C) sours the euphoria; the genre
> darkens synthpop → cinematic underneath. Resolves down into verse3's bleak E-minor pads.

This satisfies the brief exactly (`decisions/05`): key change + same rootless trick,
resolving back onto the **5 of the original key** as the break ends. Spotlight the D# in
the final pivot bars — it is the reintroduction of the song's engine.

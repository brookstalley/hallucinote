## The moment

D#–F#–A, the leading tone at maximum pull); the coda is the long-delayed **V → i
cadence, finally with roots** — the first true grounding. Idiom returns to **cinematic**

**Found = located, not happy.**

## The triple convergence (one instant, ~bar 113)

2. **Sub / timbral:** the **sub-osc E** swells up over ~4 bars (bars 113–116) — the first

4. **Melodic:** the **melody lands on E** for the first time, held (`decisions/06`).

Voice, floor, pulse, and harmony all arrive on the same beat.

## Bookend with the intro

The E that swells up here is the **same E** whose power-chord tail was dying in the intro
(`decisions/12`). Present-but-vanishing (intro) → absent (whole song) → present-and-
arriving (coda). Consider **reusing the intro's reverb space** so the two ends rhyme
sonically: the room that was ringing as you arrived too late rings again as you finally
land. "You just missed it" → "…you made it."

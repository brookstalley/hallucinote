# Condense Beethoven's 5th into 2 minutes of punk

## Question
What is this song actually doing?

## Answer
Take the *chord progressions* of Beethoven's 5th Symphony and compress them into a 2-minute punk-rock song. Not a cover, not a parody — a translation. The four-note fate motif and the symphony's tragic-to-triumphant emotional arc are the load-bearing ideas; everything else (orchestration, length, dynamic shape) gets rewritten in the punk idiom.

## Rationale
The user's prompt is the artistic constraint: "condense the chord progressions of Beethoven's 5th into those 2 minutes." This is the through-line every other decision serves. When compose-time choices conflict, the tiebreaker is: does it preserve the harmonic skeleton + emotional arc of the symphony, performed with punk-rock energy? If yes, keep it. If no, cut it.

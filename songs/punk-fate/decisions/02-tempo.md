# Tempo: 180 BPM

## Question
What tempo serves a 2-minute punk-rock song?

## Answer
180 BPM (quarter pulse, Live's default tempo basis).

## Rationale
- **Genre fit.** Punk rock sits in the 160–200 BPM band; 180 is squarely "fast and driving" without crossing into hardcore/thrash territory. The Ramones, Buzzcocks, early Clash all live in this neighborhood.
- **Length math.** At 180 BPM in 4/4, 2 minutes ≈ 90 bars. That comfortably fits a 7-section arc (intro/verse/chorus/verse/chorus/bridge/outro) with the default 8-bar sections from the scaffold, plus room to lengthen verses/choruses to 16 bars where the harmonic motion needs it.
- **Energy.** "Real punk energy" was an explicit user direction — at 160 BPM the song would feel polite; at 200 it would feel hardcore. 180 is the punk sweet spot.

## What this binds
- Section bar counts (see `06-structure.md`) — total bars must land near 90 to hit 2 minutes
- Drum pattern density at compose time (driving 8ths on hats, backbeat on 2/4)

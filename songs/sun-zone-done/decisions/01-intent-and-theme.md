# Intent & Theme

**Question:** What is this song about, and what's the deliverable?

**Answer:** A reggae × speed-metal mashup with alternating (not overlapping) sections, expressing the tension between wanting to relax and having too much to do — and how the two states reinforce each other ("I have a lot to do because I was lazy; I want to be lazy because I'm worn out from doing so much"). Fun but **not a novelty song** — the music has to actually work and the harmony has to carry the meaning.

**Decided by:** user

**Rationale:**
The two genres are antithetical on every musical axis (tempo, articulation, harmonic mood, body posture). That contrast IS the song. Alternating sections — not overlapping — means each genre gets to be itself fully, and the *transitions* carry the dramatic weight. The lyric sketch the user gave ("chillin in the sun zone, rasta vibes flowing in the — NO TIME FOR THAT GOTTA GET STUFF DONE") establishes the rhetorical structure: relaxation interrupted, mid-sentence, by urgency.

Success criteria (paraphrased from the brief):
1. **Fun** — listener smiles, not cringes.
2. **Expresses its point** — the conflict comes through without explanation.
3. **Sounds GREAT** — production is clean, not muddy at transitions; both genre halves are convincing.

# Section Structure

**Question:** What's the song's form?

**Answer:** 8 sections, ~2:30 finished length.

```
intro    →  verse1   →  chorus1  →  verse2   →  chorus2  →  bridge  →  chorus3  →  outro
8 bars      16 bars     8 bars      16 bars     8 bars      12 bars    8 bars      8 bars
```

**Decided by:** inferred from genre + brief; not yet challenged by user

**Rationale:**

**Intro (8 bars):** The song's thesis in microcosm. Drifting reggae (organ + sparse skank, no drums) for 4 bars → one-drop drum enters with bass for 2 bars → **1 bar metal WHACK** (E5-F5 stab + double-kick burst + lead voice F♮ scream) → 1 bar reggae groove resuming into Verse 1. The listener learns the alternation rule before any vocal arrives.

**Verses (16 bars each):** Two 8-bar cycles of [4 bars reggae / 4 bars metal]. Verse 1 establishes the call-and-response ("chillin in the sun zone / NO TIME FOR THAT"). Verse 2 ratchets up — same structure, denser instrumentation, lead voice gets more rhythmically aggressive.

**Choruses (8 bars):** Faster alternation — [2 bars reggae / 2 bars metal] × 2 cycles. The counter-melody lead carries a hook above the placeholder vocal. Chorus 3 (after the bridge) varies — maybe extends to 12 bars, ends on a metal payoff or a reggae release.

**Bridge (12 bars):** Where the alternation rule gets broken. Two ideas to try in build:
- Genre bleed: metal vocal cadence + power chords with reggae bass underneath
- The "I'm tired because I overworked because I was lazy" loop: each 4-bar phrase morphs the previous, blurring genre identity
The bridge is the song's emotional crux — the moment it admits the two states aren't opposites but the same loop.

**Outro (8 bars):** Reverse of intro. Metal fading, settling into reggae, drifting out. Relaxation "wins" but ambiguously — we're back where we started. Optionally end on a single sustained Em9 with no resolution, or a final F♯→F♮ slide that hangs unresolved.

**Total runtime estimate at 180 BPM:** ~84 bars total = ~112 seconds = **~1:55**. May extend choruses or add a half-time breakdown if it feels short.

**Forbidden:**
- No tempo changes (locked in `04-tempo-and-subdivision.md`).
- No key changes (locked in `03-key-and-modal-pivot.md`).
- No within-section meter changes (Hallucinote constraint W10-H).
- No simultaneous overlapping of reggae and metal (locked in `02-genre-alternation.md`) — except in the bridge, where the bleed is the *point*.
